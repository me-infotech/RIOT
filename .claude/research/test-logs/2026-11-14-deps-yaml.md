## Limits

30 of 756 dependency files (those of hello-world and gnrc_networking);
all others stay in Make syntax (mixed operation is covered by the parity
above).
The one-time export still uses the translator of the proof of concept;
`riot_deps.py` imports it from `build-poc/resolver`. Generated files
lose the original layout (blank lines, indentation, line breaks of long
lists); the generated text is normalised (one assignment per line where
it exceeds 80 columns). `$(call echowarn,…)` messages are written back as
`$(warning …)` / `$(info …)` (same meaning, different colour output).
